Market Assumptions · April 2026
Why We Believe the Numbers,
and Where We Stay Honest
This document frames the market context behind every figure in the forecast engine. We are building income verification infrastructure at the intersection of private credit, fintech, and blockchain — three of the fastest-moving spaces in global finance. The assumptions below are grounded in publicly available data, early commercial traction, and a clear-eyed view of what still needs to be proven.
Droplinked — US E-Commerce Private Credit
Market The Opportunity

Global e-commerce sales are projected to surpass $7 trillion by 2027, with Shopify holding approximately $155.91 billion in market cap as of April 2026 and commanding roughly 1-in-3 US stores. The United States alone hosts over 228,000 Shopify merchants, with significant concentration in California (50k), New York (20k), Florida (18k), and Texas (16k). Our entry point is merchants generating over $1M in GMV with at least 2 years of operating history — businesses with real revenue but limited access to traditional credit due to the nature of their income.

The private credit market globally is valued at over $1.7 trillion and is actively shifting toward blockchain infrastructure. Total onchain private credit has surged 133% since 2023, moving from $25 million in early 2025 to an estimated $1.675 billion in active loan market capitalization as of March 2026. We are entering this market at the exact moment institutional appetite for off-chain data-backed lending is peaking.

Founder Signal Why Droplinked

Droplinked's founder has already demonstrated commercial conviction: the company crossed $2 million in referrals to capital financiers in Q4 2025 — operating only in the UAE, only on the traditional lending side. They hold a $100M credit line in the MENA region, maintain a close relationship with PayPal MENA, and are actively expanding into the US market as their next growth vector. This is not a company pitching a concept. It is a company with proof of distribution, now entering a larger market.

Our partnership with Droplinked is further validated by parallel relationships being formed with Textile (Israel), Tribeca Capital (New York), and TradeVu (Nigeria) — confirming that demand for onchain income-verified private credit is a global, not regional, phenomenon.

Macro Tailwind The Onchain Moment

The current US political environment is actively positioning America as the crypto capital of the world. For Cr3dentials, this creates a rare alignment: we are bringing verifiable, privacy-preserving business income data onchain at the moment regulators are building frameworks to support it. Senior tranches in onchain private credit currently offer 8–10% annualized APY — attractive to institutional capital that is increasingly looking beyond over-collateralized crypto lending into real-world asset-backed yield.

Our approach — using off-chain income data to extend credit onchain — directly addresses the saturation in collateral-based DeFi lending and opens the door for global investors to participate in US small business financing.

Kasi Money — South Africa Gig Economy
Market The Underrated Opportunity

South Africa's gig economy is valued at approximately $230 million in 2025 and growing. Kasi Money's own trajectory tells the story clearly: they started 2025 with 55,000 drivers and closed the year with over 100,000 drivers — nearly doubling in 12 months. Uber captures roughly 40% of the ride-hailing driver base in their user profile, giving us a target of ~40,000 drivers to start as we move out of the pilot phase into production-scale verification.

Kasi Money's plans to expand beyond Uber to additional localized platforms means our integration is not a single-platform bet — it is a foundational layer in their growth operations. Every new platform they onboard is additional verification volume for Cr3dentials.

Paycrest — African Crypto On/Off Ramp
Market Payments at Scale

The crypto on/off ramp market is projected to grow to $11.7 billion by 2033. Paycrest operates in Nigeria — crypto's number one market by transaction volume globally — and crossed $2 million in transaction volume in Q3–Q4 2025. The market validation extends further: Kotani Pay, another African payments partner we are onboarding, averages $15 million in transaction volume on a good month.

Our 7% revenue share with a $200 per-transaction cap means we are aligned with high-value transactions. At a $10,000 average transaction, we earn $200 per transaction — already at the cap — meaning our revenue is determined entirely by volume, not transaction size. The key variable is transaction count, which is why our bear scenario starts conservatively at 5 transactions per month.

The Macro Case — Why Now

Cr3dentials is not building a single product for a single market. We are building verification infrastructure that sits between traditional income data and onchain credit systems — a layer that does not yet exist at scale but is actively demanded by every partner we speak to.

The convergence happening right now — $1.7 trillion in private credit moving toward blockchain rails, Shopify merchants underserved by traditional lenders, African payment infrastructure maturing, and the US regulatory environment opening to crypto — creates a window that will not stay open indefinitely. We are positioned early, with real contracts, real traction, and a model that generates revenue from day one without requiring speculative upside to be worth the investment.

The numbers in this forecast engine represent a grounded view of what we can see. The numbers we cannot yet model — Textile, Tribeca, TradeVu, Kotani Pay at scale — represent the upside that will make the base case look conservative in retrospect.

Key Risks to Monitor

Droplinked US expansion timeline — deal flow depends on successful US market entry and loan batch initiation. Delays push revenue rightward, not downward.

Kasi verification ramp — production scale is near but not yet realized. The minimum fee provides a revenue floor while volume builds.

Paycrest bank integrations — transaction volume is directly tied to the number of supported Nigerian banks. Each integration adds volume non-linearly.

Regulatory environment — onchain private credit regulation is evolving globally. We view current momentum as favorable but monitor closely.
